# Weight-Space Steering in Diffusion Models: A Public Lab Notebook

> **Scope limit**: Nothing in this notebook reaches outside Krea-2 or outside one narrow corner of image space. All 40 prompts are upper-body or close-up character portraits and all 40 contain the phrases "bold ink outlines" and "hatched shadows". Treat any exploratory effect size here as an upper bound: three consecutive confirmation rounds returned between a third and a half of their exploratory estimates. See [docs/scope.md](docs/scope.md).

## How to read the table below

Every claim in this notebook carries one of four words, and they are not a ranking. Three of
them are outcomes; the fourth deliberately is not.

* **Holds** — tested against a criterion fixed before the number was looked at, and it passed.
* **Ambiguous** — tested, and the result does not decide. The threshold was declared in advance
  and the evidence landed beside it, or two measurements of the same thing disagree. An
  ambiguous claim is not a weak *holds*; nothing may be built on it.
* **Overturned** — tested and falsified. Most of these were this project's own predictions.
  They are kept on the page that made them, next to what replaced them.
* **Open** — *not an outcome.* A question posed and not answered: the experiment was not run,
  the control was not rendered, or the design cannot separate the two explanations on offer.
  An open claim with a lot of numbers beside it is still open.

Each row links to the page that makes the claim. Every page answers the same five questions in
the same order — what it is in two minutes, the verdict, why I might be wrong, the data, and
where everything came from — so the case against a result is never further away than the case
for it.

## The rules this notebook runs on

* **Every published number comes from a file in `data/`.** If a figure exists only in prose, it
  is measured and persisted before it is published. Numbers that turned out to have no file
  behind them are named as such on the page that carried them, not quietly deleted.
* **Every figure is registered in `experiments/figures.yaml` and drawn by a script that reads a
  measurement.** Captions are derived from the row being drawn, so a label cannot contradict
  the data. No figure crops to fixed pixel coordinates.
* **`README.md` and `index.html` are generated.** `notebook/*.md` is the source;
  `python experiments/build_notebook.py` writes the table above and the site. Editing either by
  hand is reverted by the next build.
* **The contract is enforced.** `python experiments/validate_notebook.py` checks the structure
  of every page, the statuses against the stages, the anchors, the figure registry and the
  reproduction blocks against `data/`. A claim marked *holds* on an exploratory page is
  rejected.
* **Mistakes stay on the page.** Corrections are written where the error was published,
  including the ones this project made about its own instruments.
