**H2** — Write `experiments/arm_identifiability.py` per §2 to §6. The three things that
decide whether this study is worth anything:

- **Leave one *prompt* out**, 24 folds. Random cross-validation is forbidden — it leaks the

- **The null is the permutation of §5**, arm labels exchanged within each (prompt, seed)
  group, the whole leave-one-prompt-out procedure re-run, 1000 times, `random.Random(1337)`.
  **A binomial test against 1/6 must not appear anywhere in this script or its output** —
  the 720 predictions are not independent.

  yourself trying a third, stop and report it as an unplanned exploration instead.

accuracy (recall more than 3x the mean of the others) and whether one fold carries it

reported as **carried by one arm** or **fragile**, and no claim is promoted.
