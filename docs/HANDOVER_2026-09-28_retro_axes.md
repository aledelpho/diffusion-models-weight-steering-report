## 1. Why this is worth doing, in one paragraph

Two axes were added on 2026-09-28: **structure coherence** (is what is left still a drawing?) and
**energy at each scale of detail** (what size is the texture that changed?). Every earlier statistic
in this project answers only *how far the image moved*. In one day the two new axes overturned a
published ranking, found that the project's default dose sits past a cliff, and showed that a
chroma claim was about redistribution rather than removal. Every bench in the corpus was read
without them. The absolute values now exist for all of them; what is missing is the join to each
bench's baseline.

## 3. What to produce, per bench

One script `experiments/retro_<bench>_reading.py` and one result document.

1. **Parse the filename into (prompt family, condition, seed)** with an explicit regular
   expression, and **print how many files failed to parse.** Then write the arithmetic line that
   balances: the atlas's is `624 perturbed + 24 baselines + 1 determinism check = 649`. A silent
   parse failure is a silently dropped condition.
2. **Ratios against the matching baseline** — same prompt family, same seed, never a pooled one:
   `coherence_ratio`, `band0_ratio` … `band4_ratio`, `peak_band`, `contrast_ratio`, `chroma_ratio`.
3. **Four readings:**
   * **Does the coherence axis discriminate here?** Report its spread and the fraction of cells
     below 0.90. On the atlas it discriminates on photo, watercolour and charcoal (spread
     0.09–0.10, a quarter of cells below 0.90) and is **near-blind on lowpoly, claymation, ukiyo-e
     and pixel** (spread 0.04, 1 %), whose baseline coherence sits near a floor. **A bench where
     nothing moves is telling you the axis cannot see that style — say that, do not report a clean
     null.**
   * **Is there a knee?** If the bench has a dose or strength ladder, report the change in
     coherence **per unit of log-dose**, step by step, not the raw values — the steps are not equal
     and the raw table hides it. On the map the last step is 12×, 23× and 3.3× the previous for
     `Block_5 pos`, `Block_6 pos` and `Block_6 neg`, and it is *not* the largest dose ratio.
   * **What is the scale signature of each condition?** The shape of the band profile is the
     finding, never its total: a monotone tilt, a mid-scale hump, a mid-scale hole and a notch at
     2–8 px are four different operations that a single texture number reports as one.
   * **Which conditions move the image and keep the drawing** (coherence ≥ 0.98) and which move it
     and lose it.
4. **Then the question that pays for the work.** Take the claims that bench's published document
   makes and ask whether any was made on a condition the coherence axis now calls a collapse, or at
   a dose past a knee. **Name them in a list and stop.** No claim changes status in this work.

## 4. Traps, all of them paid for on 2026-09-28

* **Cross-check any estimator you write against an independent implementation before publishing
  from it.** Two defects were found this way in one hour, and neither was visible from a single
  implementation: a 9 × 9 window mean built as a summed-area table in float32 loses **0.9 % per
  window** to catastrophic cancellation and moved a headline condition from 12th of 80 to 1st; and
  `np.convolve(..., mode="same")` **zero-pads**, so on a constant image — true band energy zero —
  it reports a quarter of a real render's band-0 energy. Use `scipy.ndimage.uniform_filter` and
  `convolve1d(..., mode="nearest")`. `experiments/retro_axes_crosscheck.py` is the pattern: it
  exits non-zero when two implementations disagree.
* **Do not divide the two axes by one another** to make a single score. A displacement-over-damage
  ratio puts a quantity that can approach zero in the denominator; this project got that wrong
  three times in one day. Rank by Pareto dominance, or report both columns.
* **A whole-frame chroma reading cannot tell removal from redistribution.** `Block_6 pos` reads
  1.488 on the frame and 0.726 on the object, because it multiplies the *background's* chroma by
  12.5 while draining the object's. Every bench in this list except `colour_binding` is a full
  scene with no object mask, so **report chroma as a whole-frame quantity and say so in those
  words.**
* **The unit is the condition, not the render** (pitfall 17). Pool seeds before any test.
* **A block can be two-sided.** `blk27` is 75th of 80 pushed negative and the largest
  line-preserving move of all 56 single-block conditions pushed positive. Never summarise a block
  by one arm.
* **Quote a rank only at the resolution it supports.** When the precision defect was repaired, 26
  of 80 conditions moved more than five places. Report the top and bottom of a ranking, not
  positions 30 to 50.
