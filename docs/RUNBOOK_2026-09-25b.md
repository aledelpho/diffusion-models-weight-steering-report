## BLOCK D — The mountain, phase 2

### D0 — Read this before anything else

`docs/prereg_mountain_reachability.md` phase 2 specifies **384 baselines, no tuner in the
graph**. That grows the base cloud `B` against which `d_out` is measured. It does **not**
add a single prompt to the test.

The test's unit of analysis is the prompt of the *edited* images, and that is still the
**eight** stage-9 style prompts S1–S8. Phase 2 as written therefore improves the quality
of `d_out` and leaves the statistical power exactly where it is: a sign-flip floor of
`2/2^8 = 0.0078`, which after Holm across four arms gives a best achievable p of `0.031`.
`preset_pos_2x` already reached 7 prompts out of 8 and missed by one.

Anyone who reports that phase 2 "fixes the power problem" is wrong. It does not.

There are therefore two variants, and **the choice between them is Alessandro's alone.**
Do not begin either without his explicit instruction naming the variant.

### D1 — Phase 2 exactly as pre-registered

Worth doing on its own merits: `d_out` is a minimum over the base cloud, and a sparse
cloud inflates it.

* 24 prompts spread across the families already in `data/prompts.json` — coverage of the
  mountain, not 24 variations of one scene
* 16 seeds each: `42, 777, 1337, 9999, 4242145, 101..110`
* **384 renders**, folder `benchmark_montagna`, naming
  `{prompt_id}_baseline_seed{seed}_00001_.png`
* same sampler as every other bench: euler_ancestral, 9 steps, cfg 1.0, simple,
  denoise 1.0, 1024x1280
* manifest at `data/montagna_images.csv`, same columns as `data/stage9_images.csv`

Then re-run `experiments/mountain_reachability.py` in full. **Nothing in §2 or §4 of the
pre-registration changes**: the corpus grows, the criteria do not. The verdict still
rests on eight test prompts and is still reported as such.

### D2 — The extension that actually raises the power

**This is not pre-registered.** It requires an amendment to
`docs/prereg_mountain_reachability.md`, written and committed **before** any render is
generated. Do not improvise it.

The power problem is solved only by adding **test** prompts — prompts on which the arms
themselves are rendered, not just baselines.

Per new test prompt: 5 seeds x (1 baseline + the 4 arms the mountain test uses:
`preset_pos_1x`, `preset_pos_2x`, `blockshuf_neg_1x`, `rand_pos_1x`) = **25 renders**.

| target | new prompts | renders | sign-flip floor | best p after Holm x4 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| clears the gate (12) | 4 | **100** | 4.88e-04 | 1.95e-03 |
| comfortable (16) | 8 | **200** | 3.05e-05 | 1.22e-04 |

D1 and D2 together: 484 or 584 renders.

**The selection rule for the new prompts is the part that can invalidate the whole
thing.** It is fixed in the amendment, before rendering, and it is mechanical — for
instance the next N prompt ids in file order within the families already represented.
The current per-prompt results are known: `preset_pos_1x` produced its largest positive
deltas on S7 (+1.53) and S8 (+1.92). **Choosing new prompts because they resemble S7 and
S8 would guarantee the result and destroy it at the same time.** The rule must be
writable down before anyone looks at which prompts would help.
