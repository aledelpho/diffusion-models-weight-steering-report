## BLOCK B5 — Diagnostics on the shared axis

**Why this block exists.** Every one of the twelve rows in `data/shared_axis.csv`
returned `keeps a direction of its own`, including both random arms. A test that
separates nothing needs its own instrument checked before any of it is written up.
Three specific quantities are missing.

**This block does not re-analyse and does not revise.** `data/shared_axis.csv`,
`data/shared_axis_loadings.csv` and `experiments/shared_axis.py` are **frozen** and
must not be modified, regenerated or overwritten. The verdicts recorded there stand as
recorded. This block measures something the pre-registration did not think to ask:
whether the axis it projected onto was distinguishable from an arbitrary direction.
That is a post-hoc diagnostic, it is labelled as one in its output, and it cannot
change a recorded verdict.

**B5.1** — Write `experiments/shared_axis_diagnostics.py`. It reads the existing data
and writes one new file. It imports nothing from and writes nothing over Block B's
outputs.

**B5.2 — D1, the angle between the two axes.** Report, as plain cosines:

- `cos(u_stage7, u_stage9_all_arms)`;
- `cos(u_stage7, u_loo[arm])` for each of the six arms;
- `cos(u_loo[arm_i], u_loo[arm_j])` for every pair.

Context for why this matters: the top loadings of the two definitions barely overlap,
and on `fft_radial_slope` they carry opposite signs (`-0.149933` on stage 7,
`+0.372862` on the stage-9 mean). If the primary and secondary axes are close to
orthogonal, the primary analysis was projecting onto a direction with no relation to
the thing it was meant to measure.

**B5.3 — D2, the random-direction baseline for `share`.** This is the important one.

For each arm, draw 1000 unit vectors uniformly on the unit sphere in 23 dimensions
(normalise i.i.d. standard Gaussians; `random.Random(1337)`; record the seed), and for
each of them compute `share` by **exactly the same code path** `shared_axis.py` uses —
mean over cells of `|cos(Delta_cell, u)|`. Report, per arm:

- the observed `share` against `u_stage7` and against `u_loo`;
- the mean and 95th percentile of the 1000 random-direction shares;
- the percentile at which the observed `share` falls in that distribution.

The arithmetic to expect: for a uniformly random unit vector in 23 dimensions the
expected `|cos|` against any fixed vector is `sqrt(2 / (pi * 23)) = 0.1664`, with RMS
`1/sqrt(23) = 0.2085`. The observed shares in `data/shared_axis.csv` run from `0.1392`
to `0.3213` with a mean near `0.23`. **If the observed shares fall below the 95th
percentile of the random-direction distribution, the shared axis explains no more of
each edit than an arbitrary direction would, and Block B cannot support a published
claim.** Report that in those words if that is what comes out. Do not soften it and do
not rescue it.

**B5.4 — D3, the null with a matched cell count.** The arms have 40 cells
(8 prompts x 5 seeds). The sign-randomised null is built from baseline seed pairs:
8 prompts x C(5,2) = **80 cells**, and each baseline appears in four different pairs, so
the null's cells are neither equal in number nor independent of one another.

Cosine is scale-free, so this may well not matter. Verify it rather than assuming it:
rebuild the null using 40 cells drawn as **disjoint** baseline pairs (each baseline used
at most once per draw), 200 draws, same procedure otherwise, and report `null_mean` and
`null_p95` beside the 80-cell values already recorded. If `null_p95` moves materially,
say so; the 40-cell figure is then the one that matters and every arm's verdict has to
be re-read against it — **reported, not rewritten.**

**B5.5** — Write `data/shared_axis_diagnostics.csv`. It must carry a column marking
every row as a post-hoc diagnostic, so that no later reader mistakes it for part of the
pre-registered analysis.

**B5.6** — Validator at zero errors. Commit script and data together.

Suggested message: `diagnostica dell'asse condiviso: angolo tra le definizioni, share contro direzioni casuali, nullo a celle appaiate`

**STOP and report.** Give the three diagnostics verbatim, and state plainly whether
D2 leaves Block B publishable.
