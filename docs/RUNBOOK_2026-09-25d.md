**I2 — the stage-7 CIELAB moments.** Extend `experiments/measure_palette.py` so it produces,

`data/palette_position.csv` and `data/palette_instrument_check*.csv` are **frozen**. Do not
modify, regenerate or overwrite them. Write stage 7 to its own file.

**I3 — the regional measurement.** Implement §3: three luminance bands, cut points taken from
the **baseline** image's `L*` terciles and applied unchanged to the edited image, so that a
region which darkens moves band and the movement is recorded. Per band: mean `L*`, mean `a*`,
mean `b*`, and the pixel fraction — 12 features. Write `data/colour_features_regional.csv`.

Run it once at 3 bands and once at 5 (§3 sensitivity). 3 is primary.

**I4 — the classification.** Write `experiments/colour_identifiability.py`.

**Reuse Block H's machinery unchanged** — the same leave-one-prompt-out folds, the same
permutation null of arm labels within each (prompt, seed) group, 1000 draws,
`random.Random(1337)`, the same two classifiers with no tuning. Import it or copy it
verbatim, but do not re-derive it: the whole value of this study is that its numbers are
comparable with `data/arm_identifiability_tests.csv`, and any change to the procedure
destroys that. **No binomial test anywhere.**

Feature sets per §2: T (19 texture), C1, C2, C3. Tests per §5, in the stated order, with the
primary being **C2, `preset_pos` versus `rand_pos`, nearest centroid, stage 7**.

**I5 — the comparison that decides what this study means.** Accuracy of **T** against accuracy
of **T + C3**, on the **identical folds**. Report it beside the tests and do not Holm-correct
it — it is a comparison of accuracies, not a test against a null.

This is the step that separates "colour decodes the arm" from "colour carries something of
its own". Colour features can score well merely by correlating with texture ones. Do not
skip it and do not report the colour-only accuracy without it in the same table.

**I6 — the guards.** Apply §7 before any verdict: one arm's recall above three times the mean
of the others -> **carried by one arm**; one fold carrying more than 40% of the above-chance
correct predictions -> **fragile**. Either one, and no claim is promoted.

**I7** — Apply §6 as written, including the outcome where C3 is significant and C2 is not
(the signal is regional and global aggregates destroy it) and the outcome where colour is
significant but adds nothing to texture. Both are results and both get recorded as verdicts.

**I8** — Run the stage-9 replication. Separate rows, never pooled with stage 7.

**I9** — Validator at zero errors. Commit. No page, no figure, no claim.
