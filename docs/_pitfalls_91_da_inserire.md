## 91 — blind yes/no questions written in the negative

**What happened.** C78 amendments 4–6 asked eight questions about the absence of a defect ("L'orco non ha armatura
di metallo?", "Nessun altro ha le corna?"). To confirm an absence the labeller had to answer "sì" through a double
negation, on hundreds of pictures. Agreement held (32 of 32 repeats), but Alessandro called the wording a trap that
cost attention on every answer.

**Rule.** Ask every question in the positive, about what is visible ("L'orco ha le corna?"), and turn the answer
into error / no error in the tally script.
