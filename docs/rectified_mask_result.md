# Rectified sign masks on `Block_4` and `Block_6` — outcome

**Date**: 2026-09-28 · **Design**: [`RENDERS_2026-09-27_rectified_masks.md`](RENDERS_2026-09-27_rectified_masks.md),
frozen before the renders · **Renders**: 72, launched by Alessandro, `benchmark_rectified_masks`
· **Measurement**: `experiments/measure_rectified_masks.py` →
`data/rectified_mask_vector_check.csv`, `data/rectified_mask_measurements.csv` (72 rows),
`data/rectified_mask_verdict.csv` (24 rows), `data/rectified_mask_stats.csv`
· **No render was generated by the analyst.**

---

## 0. The question, and the short answer

Alessandro's idea: if a slider is a bundle of sub-knobs and some of them push the opposite way,
**flip the opposed ones and the slider should get stronger and cleaner**. `Block_4` (opposed on
contrast) and `Block_6` (opposed on grain) were the two places where that could be tested, because
the pre-check had found members that actually reverse when their gain reverses.

**Short answer: the arithmetic the idea rests on does not hold, so the idea was not tested.** One
of the four composition predictions is not merely off, it lands on the wrong side of 1 by 0.31.
The design said in advance what to do in that case, and this document does it.

## 1. Provenance of the renders

## 3. M5b must not be read as "the mask does not work"

The spec pre-committed, in writing, before the renders existed:

> **M5 is the experiment.** M1–M4 are the arithmetic it rests on; if they fail, additivity at
> sub-block level fails and M5 means nothing whatever it says.

**M4 failed.** `B6_anti` was predicted at 0.882 and came in at 1.191 — not a miss of degree, a miss
of direction. The prediction is the product of two single-block measurements taken on
`benchmark_profondita` at the same dose, and it is wrong by 0.31. So the composition rule that
generates every number in the "predicted" column is not reliable on this group, and M5b — which is
a difference between two conditions whose predictions were built the same way — inherits that
unreliability.

The honest statement is therefore **not** "the anti-mask beat the mask on grain". It is: **at
sub-block granularity in `Block_6`, composing two single-block effects does not predict their
joint effect, and the experiment that needed that composition to hold cannot be read.**

The one thing M5 does deliver is on contrast, where the arithmetic did hold (M2 confirmed, M1 grey
but on the right side): `B4_mask` 1.043 against `B4_anti` 0.931, a gap of **+0.113** in the
predicted direction, at identical Frobenius displacement. That is a real separation on a clean
control — it is just one of the two the design asked for.

## 4. What the 24 cells do say: the sign is predicted, the size is not

Composition gets the **side of 1** right for every mask condition and half the anti-mask ones:

| | side of 1 correct | |
|---|--:|---|
| masks (opposed signs) | **12/12** | all four statistics, both arms, all three mask conditions |
| anti-masks (concordant signs) | **6/12** | a coin flip |

Fisher exact between the two groups, **p = 0.0137**. Three deflating controls, all in
`data/rectified_mask_stats.csv`, because that number is more fragile than it looks:

1. **The combined arm is not independent.** `B4B6` is built from the same blocks as `B4` and `B6`,
   so 8 of the 24 cells are not new information. Dropping them: **8/8 against 4/8, Fisher
   p = 0.077.** Not significant.
2. **The coin-flip null is wrong.** Most edits push these statistics below 1 anyway. The naive rule
   "always predict below 1" already scores **9/12** on the mask cells and **6/12** on the anti
   cells — that is, it matches composition exactly on the anti cells and loses only three on the
   masks.
3. **So the whole informative content is three cells.** The mask conditions where composition
   predicted *above* 1, against the general downward tendency: **3/3 correct, binomial p = 0.125.**

**Reported honestly, this is a lead and not a result.** Composition predicts direction better for
opposed sub-blocks than for concordant ones, at p = 0.077 on the independent cells. The asymmetry
points the way the angle rule (ρ = 0.939 − 0.278·cos) would point, and it is measured at sub-block
granularity where that rule has never been measured — which is register item **C12** — but it does
not clear a threshold and must not be cited as though it did.

What **is** solid in the same table is the failure mode: the deviations from prediction are not
scattered, they are concentrated in the concordant conditions and they all go the same way.
`B6_anti_pos` is +0.357 on contrast and +0.309 on grain; `B4B6_anti_pos` is +0.344 and +0.398.
Four of the five largest deviations in the table are concordant-sign conditions overshooting
upward. **Pushing two sub-blocks the same way does something that pushing them opposite ways does
not, and composition does not know about it.**

## 5. One pair, looked at rather than measured

Alessandro picked out two images from the corpus without knowing their labels and described them as
"flat colours / synthesis" against "shaded colours / realism". Identified by perceptual hash
against the renders on disk, exact match: they are `P01_B4_mask_pos_seed777` and
`P01_B4_mask_neg_seed777` — **the same mask, the two opposite arms**.

| | contrast | grain |
|---|--:|--:|
| `B4_mask` pos | 0.9117 | **1.1217** |
| `B4_mask` neg | 0.9142 | **0.7535** |

(`data/rectified_mask_measurements.csv`, rows `P01,777,B4_mask`.)

**Contrast identical to 0.3 %, grain apart by 49 %.** In that cell, reversing the mask's relative
sign moves grain and leaves contrast where it was. Mean saturation **0.3812** against **0.3125** and mean gradient
**0.03329** against **0.02966**, same file — the arm read as "synthetic" is the more saturated and
harder-edged one.

This is one cell of six and is not offered as a measurement; the pooled figures in §2 are the
measurement. It is recorded because the observer's two categories and the two statistics separated
the same pair the same way, and because a mask whose two arms differ on one axis and not the other
is what §3 of the design was hoping for and M6 did not find.

## 6. Consequences

* **The sign-mask idea is not refuted. It is untested**, because its arithmetic prerequisite failed
  on `Block_6`. A test needs a composition rule measured on the combination itself rather than
  predicted from singles.
* **`Block_6` at sub-block level is where composition breaks.** M4 is the largest single failure of
  additivity recorded in this project at any granularity.
* **The register gains an item**: measure the joint effect of sub-block pairs directly, at both
  relative signs, before any further mask is designed on composed predictions.
* Nothing here changes the status of a published claim.
