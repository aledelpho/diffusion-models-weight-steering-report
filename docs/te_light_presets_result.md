## Reading

* At the same picture change, four layers at about ±0.60 are **not** more coherent across subjects than one layer
  pushed (rule 1, −0.08; the comparison has only 4 single-layer cards). The idea that spreading the edit buys
  coherence is not supported here.
* What varies is the preset: some are coherent at both seeds (P05, P06, P12), some never (P03, P07, P10). Coherence is
  a property of the particular preset, as in C104–C106, not of spreading.
* The change of a preset carries from the comic portraits to the other images better than chance (rule 2), as C105
  found for single edits.
* The nominal ±0.30 was too light to reach the target: the presets needed about ±0.60 per layer.

## Limits

cards; random presets, not chosen by eye; P04 and P12 cards have four subjects only; the amendment changed the cards
during the blind pass (no earlier answer used).
