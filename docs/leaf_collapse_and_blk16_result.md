# Bench 1 — the achromatic leaf is real, reproducible, and far narrower than expected

## 1. The verdicts

| # | prediction | observed | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **L2** | no unperturbed baseline collapses on its own | **0/20** | **confirmed — the edit is the cause** |
| **L1** | ≥ 2/20 on fresh seeds | **9/20** | **confirmed** |
| **L3** | ≥ 2 of 5 other wordings | **0/5**, 0/15 cells | **falsified — wording-specific** |
| **L4** | ≥ 2 of 4 other subjects | **0/4**, 0/36 cells | ~~falsified — leaf-specific~~ **NOT TESTED** — see `looking_at_the_leaf_corpus.md` §1 |
| **L5** | prototypical < unusual < undeclared | **0/4** subjects | **falsified — and reversed** |

L2 was read first, as the spec required. It passes, so the rest is readable.

## 2. It is not a singleton. It is a switch, and it fires about half the time

Arm A, chroma ratio against each seed's own baseline, sorted:

```
0.034 0.037 0.037 0.038 0.043 0.044 0.049 0.082 0.085 | 0.518 0.602 0.948 0.965
1.005 1.016 1.115 1.117 1.164 1.230 1.311
```

**Nine cells sit between 0.034 and 0.085. The tenth is 0.518.** There is nothing in between. This
is a discrete mode that either fires or does not, not a continuum of desaturation, and when it
fires it takes 92–97 % of the colour while leaving the object in place (IoU 0.74 to 0.96,
mean 0.88). Seed 2001 reproduces the original almost exactly: the same leaf, the same veins, no
colour.

**A correction to my own reading of five hours ago.** `colour_gate_and_chroma_audit.md` §4 called
this "a singleton, one cell in 108" and filed it as a lead rather than a finding. That used the
whole corpus as the denominator when the event is conditional on a cell: of the 108 cells only
**three** were `LN` × `Block_4 neg`, and one of those three fired. 1/3 then, 9/20 now — the same
rate, about 40 %, and it was never rare. **Drafted defect 81: taking the base rate from the whole
corpus when the event is conditional on one cell of it understates it by the number of cells you
divided by.**

## 3. And yet it survives neither a rewording nor a change of subject

| arm | n | mean chroma ratio | lowest cell |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| **A** — same prompt, 20 fresh seeds | 20 | 0.572 | **0.034** |
| **B** — five other wordings, same subject, no colour declared | 15 | 0.964 | 0.607 |
| **C** — four other subjects, same sentence frame | 36 | 0.997 | 0.654 |

**Not one cell outside arm A comes anywhere near the threshold.** The lowest in 51 cells is 0.607,
against a mode that lives below 0.085. Changing "centred on a plain light grey background, macro
photograph, sharp focus, even studio lighting" to any of five near-synonyms — including one that
changes only the background from light grey to white — removes it completely. So does swapping the
leaf for a mushroom, a tomato, a pinecone or a banana — **but that last clause was withdrawn on
2026-09-28**: for all four of those subjects, not naming the colour gives the same picture as naming
the prototypical one (0.9° to 9.9° apart, against 52.2° for the leaf), so there was no free
inference in them to break. Arm C tested a population that could not show the effect.
See `looking_at_the_leaf_corpus.md` §1.

This kills the interpretation the experiment was built to test. **There is no general
colour/object dissociation here.** What there is, is a specific and strongly reproducible
interaction between one exact prompt string, one subject, and one edit — an unstable point in the
model, not a knob on a concept. Alessandro's dividing line between "leaf" and "change of colour",
already negative at block granularity (`colour_gate_and_chroma_audit.md` §8), does not reappear
here at any of the three levels tested.

## 4. L5 does not merely fail, it reverses — and it takes `C15` with it

Mean hue shift per subject, prototypical / unusual (purple) / undeclared:

| subject | prototypical | unusual | undeclared |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| mushroom | 0.7° | **6.0°** | 1.4° |
| tomato | 3.2° | **13.9°** | 2.6° |
| pinecone | 0.7° | **27.4°** | 2.9° |
| banana | 2.9° | **8.1°** | 2.1° |

**In all four subjects the declared unusual colour is the most mobile and the undeclared one is
nearly as pinned as the prototypical.** On the leaf the order was `LG` 4.1° < `LP` 9.4° <
`LN` 24.9°. On four new objects it is prototypical ≈ undeclared < unusual, every time.

So the `LG` < `LP` < `LN` ordering — register **C15**, carried at p = 0.039 on one arm and one
object — **is a property of that leaf prompt, not of declared colour.** It should not be cited
again without this table beside it. The one-block scope is a real limitation (all of arm C is
`Block_4 neg`), but four subjects agreeing against the leaf is not the sort of thing a second block
repairs.

# Bench 2 — `blk16` does what it was predicted to do, and the chain checks out

## 5. The verdicts

| # | prediction | observed | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **K3** | 0.200 reproduces: style 0.447 ± 0.08, coherence 1.040 ± 0.03 | **style 0.4472, coherence 1.0146** | **confirmed** |
| **K1** | coherence ≥ 0.98 at every positive dose | 0.992, 0.998, 0.995, 0.996, 1.002, **1.015** | **confirmed** |
| **K2** | style monotone with dose, positive arm | 0.045, 0.066, 0.095, 0.138, 0.194, 0.447 | **confirmed** |
| **K4** | negative arm loses line at 0.200 | 0.9829 | grey |

**K3 is the result worth the bench.** That cell already existed in `benchmark_profondita`, rendered
weeks ago from a different plan file, a different queueing script and a different drive path, and it
came back at **0.4472 against 0.447** on style. On coherence its frozen target came from a
defective estimator; recomputed on both sides with the repaired one it is **1.0146 against
1.0146** ([the defect](estimator_precision_defect.md)). The whole chain — plan
generation, queueing, tuner, sampler, file naming, measurement — is reproducible to four decimals.
That is a stronger determinism statement than `determinism_across_sessions`, because it crosses
benches rather than sessions.

## 6. `blk16` is the first edit in this project that buys style *and* line

| dose | style | coherence | cells above 1 |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 0.020 | 0.045 | 0.992 | 1/6 |
| 0.035 | 0.066 | 0.998 | 2/6 |
| 0.050 | 0.095 | 0.995 | 2/6 |
| 0.080 | 0.138 | 0.996 | 2/6 |
| 0.120 | 0.194 | 1.002 | 3/6 |
| **0.200** | **0.447** | **1.015** | **4/6** |

**Coherence rises with dose, alongside style** — not quite monotonically once the estimator is
repaired (0.998 then 0.995), but from below baseline at the lowest dose to above it at the
highest. Everywhere else in this corpus style
is bought by losing the drawn line — the five conditions this project wrongly recommended are its
five largest losses (`style_damage_frontier.md` §8c). `blk16 pos` is the opposite: the harder it is
pushed the more oriented the drawing becomes, 4 of 6 cells at the top dose.

Looked at at 100 %, as the procedure now requires: at 0.200 the ink contour is crisp, the wall
cross-hatching legible, the face well formed, the hair resolved into distinct inked curls. None of
the fibrous mush that the `Block_6` family produces.

The negative arm is not the mirror: coherence 0.983 at 0.200 and style only 0.201 against 0.447.
**`blk16` is asymmetric** — it gives much more, and much more cleanly, in one direction. K4 is grey
because it neither holds the line (≥ 1.00) nor clearly loses it (< 0.98).

One thing the ladder shows that was not predicted: **style jumps 0.194 → 0.447 between dose 0.120
and 0.200**, a factor 2.3 for a dose factor 1.67, while every step below it is close to linear.
Something changes in the top third of the range. Unregistered and unexplained; register item.

## 7. What changes

* The achromatic collapse is **confirmed and reproducible** (9/20, against 0/20 unperturbed) and
  **confined to one prompt and one subject** (0/51 elsewhere). It is an instability, not a
  mechanism.
* **`C15` is withdrawn as a general claim.** The declared/undeclared ordering reverses on four new
  subjects.
* **`blk16` is the operating point** the style/damage work was looking for, and the only edit
  measured here that raises structure coherence while moving style.
* **K3 upgrades the project's determinism claim** from within-session to across-bench.
* Drafted defect **81**: a base rate taken from the whole corpus when the event is conditional on
  one cell.
* New register items: why style is super-linear above dose 0.120 on `blk16`; and what distinguishes
  the nine seeds that fire from the eleven that do not, on identical inputs.
